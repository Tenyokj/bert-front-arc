## What Is Indexed

- Policy scheduled/cancelled/activated, including future-policy disable.
- Round-local receiver/share snapshots, eligible pledge-funded fee versus total
  fee, and actual earned accrual. Old rounds/receivers stay independent of rotation.
- Pool-to-Treasury claims, Treasury collection confirmations and actual fixed-
  beneficiary payments as distinct stages, without double counting money.
- Immutable tx-hash/log-index event records plus state, round, receiver and payee
  entities. Queries can page events; beneficiary enumeration is bounded at 16.

The original six proxy addresses/start blocks and existing V2 handlers/entities
are retained. Only candidate pool/Treasury ABI copies are synced; no candidate
dApp deployment bytecode is copied. The V3 subgraph is not modified.

Constructor `PayeeAdded` logs precede address discovery. The mapping hydrates
immutable payee getters, validates pool/token/split, then starts one template.
No block-end mutable payout getter is added to an event counter. Getter failure
or invalid metadata is explicit, and a later pool event can retry. Observation
starts at `trackingStartedAtBlock`; older Treasury payments are not backfilled.
Binding getters alone do not prove trusted runtime bytecode.

`pending = accrued - claimed`. A collection confirmation never subtracts it a
second time; a payment never creates new earnings. `observedCollected` and
`observedReleased` are observed event totals, not total lifetime receipts or
token balances. Direct donations may fund payments independently of earned
pool revenue. Missing candidate history/invalid conservation fails indexing,
rather than manufacturing zero balances. Preserve original replay start blocks.
