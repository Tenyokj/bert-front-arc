## BERT V2.4: Transparent Treasury and Earned Protocol Revenue

Arc Testnet rollout is installed: the existing FundingPool proxy now uses
implementation 2.0.0, a separate immutable ProtocolRevenueTreasury 1.0.0 is
deployed, and V2 has reopened. V3.2.1 contracts were not redeployed or modified.

- Transparent custody/reserve/revenue/surplus/shortfall accounting in dApp and Console.
- Revenue credits only from eligible earned success fees, never grant principal,
  author bonds, refunds, direct contributions, old reserve or V3 reserve inflows.
- Fixed beneficiary payouts, delayed policy changes and immutable round snapshots.
- Updated V2 Graph v0.4.0; existing source addresses/history retained.
- Populated-proxy/layout and native-Arc financial checks, local browser/Graph
  acceptance, 269 core tests and 18 rollout/config/inventory checks recorded.

### Activation Status
The approved Treasury share is 20% of eligible earned fees, not 20% of pool funds.
It is active since November 27, 2026, 10:03:38 UTC (block 74359134).
[Activation transaction](https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x08231f87e453d1f6690eb665f333a04d7d09d83dfc5d0acd62a87e35696ad109)
confirmed the exact policy; RPC and V2 Graph/client proxies agree. Existing rounds retain
their snapshots. Public accrual/collection/payment pilot remains recommended
follow-up, not completed evidence. No redeploy is needed for activation.
