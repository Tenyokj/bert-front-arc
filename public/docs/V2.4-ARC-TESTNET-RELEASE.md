## Owner Workflow After Activation

1. Activate the announced policy at/after the chain deadline. Verify its receipt
   and active receiver/share; do not open the revenue pilot round before activation.
2. A newly opened V2 round snapshots the policy. Old rounds keep their prior
   destination, including a zero Treasury share. Opening a round alone earns nothing.
3. Eligible fees vest as the winning grant actually pays out. Only the
   pledge-funded portion contributes to team revenue. Cancelled/unpaid stages
   do not create revenue; their refundable amounts retain the existing rules.
4. In `/pool`, inspect **Pending in FundingPool** for the verified Treasury.
   With a positive amount, **Collect earned revenue to this Treasury** sends
   that existing credit to the fixed Treasury, not directly to the admin wallet.
5. When the payee's **releasable** amount is positive, **Pay fixed beneficiary**
   pays `0xA4B0a01dB1d68D4CDB007F78bAB63Cb4ebd814bF`. Anyone may relay either
   action, but cannot change its destination. Each action is a real wallet
   transaction and costs native test-USDC gas; no ERC-20 approval is needed.

The two steps are not necessarily automatic. A positive accrued total is not
necessarily uncollected credit: compare pending, held, released and releasable.
Collection requires an active pool without custody shortfall. Previously
collected Treasury funds have their own payout path; issuer restrictions or a
blocked beneficiary can still prevent payment. There is no mutable payee rescue.

Example after full delivery: 100 USDC winning pledges, 5% success fee, 20% of
eligible earned fees to Treasury -> author 95 USDC, retained reserve 4 USDC,
Treasury 1 USDC. The sole beneficiary receives the Treasury's 1 USDC after
collect/release, not 20 USDC or any share of the pre-existing 1140-USDC custody.
This is testnet accounting, not real-world income, equity, APY or an investment.

After activation, send the maintainer its transaction hash. No new contract
addresses or deployment are needed. Check the successful receipt, active
Treasury/share and the corresponding indexed event. Activation neither collects
revenue nor pays a beneficiary automatically; the separate actions above do that.
